# Instagram Caption

## Format
Caption text + hashtag block.

## Length
Caption 125–300 characters visible before "more"; hook in the first line. 5–15 hashtags.

## Structure
1. Hook line.
2. 1–3 short lines of value or story.
3. CTA (save/share/comment or "link in bio").
4. Hashtag block (mix of broad + niche).

## Do
- Emoji used purposefully, line breaks for readability, a clear single CTA.

## Avoid
- Banned/irrelevant hashtags, more than 15 hashtags, burying the hook.

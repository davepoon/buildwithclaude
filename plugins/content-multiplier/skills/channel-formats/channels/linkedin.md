# LinkedIn Post

## Format
Single post, plain text. No markdown rendering — use line breaks and Unicode bullets (•) sparingly.

## Length
900–1,300 characters. First 2 lines (~140 chars) must hook before the "see more" fold.

## Structure
1. Hook line (contrarian, stat, or question).
2. One line of white space.
3. 3–6 short lines or a tight list making one point.
4. Takeaway.
5. Single CTA or question to drive comments.

## Do
- One idea per post. Conversational, first-person. 0–3 relevant hashtags at the end.

## Avoid
- Walls of text, engagement-bait ("comment YES"), more than 3 hashtags, external links in-body (put "link in comments").

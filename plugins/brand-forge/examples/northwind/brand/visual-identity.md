# Northwind — Visual Identity

tone: warm, editorial, grounded
imagery: natural light, earthy textures, wide horizons; documentary over staged

## Personality
Rooted, considered, quietly premium — a heritage outdoors brand.

## Do
- Lead with the deep green (`primary`); let the amber (`secondary`) accent, not shout.
- Keep generous margins; the wordmark needs room to breathe.

## Don't
- Recolor or outline the logo.
- Set body copy on the terracotta accent.

## Reference notes
Think field guides and national-park signage: serif headlines, calm palettes,
lots of paper-white space.
